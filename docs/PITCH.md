# Pitch and demo script

## One line

Elbasani Vepron turns the municipality's problems into a map of volunteer crews that young people can join in one tap.

## The pitch (about 2 minutes)

1. **Problem.** Young people in Elbasan want to help, but volunteering calls are scattered and villages outside the city are invisible. The municipality knows where the problems are, but has no easy way to turn a problem into a crew with a date and a meeting point.
2. **Solution.** One map for all 14 administrative units. Every pin is a concrete action with a time, a place, what to bring and how many spots are left.
3. **Loop.** Residents report problems → the youth office approves them into actions → volunteers join → crews show up → hours count toward a certificate.
4. **Safety.** Under-18s need a parent's agreement. Only the youth office sees contact details.
5. **Why it works here.** It's built around Elbasan's real geography and seasons: the Shkumbin, the Kalaja, olive harvest in Shushicë, fire risk above Labinot-Mal.

## Live demo (about 3 minutes)

1. Open the landing page. Point at the counters and the map: red pins are urgent.
2. Filter by **Environment**, then click the **Shkumbin riverbank clean-up** pin. Show the details and the crew bar.
3. Click **Join this action**, choose age **15–17** and show that the parent-consent box is required. Tick it and join: the counter and the bar go up, and the pin gets a check mark.
4. Scroll to **Report a hotspot**. Report broken glass at the Shirgjan school court. A dashed pin appears on the map.
5. Open `/admin`, enter the token, and approve the report with a title, date and crew size.
6. Back on the map, reload: the report is now a real hotspot anyone can join.

## Impact we would measure

- Volunteer hours per month, per administrative unit
- Share of actions outside the city centre
- Time from report to approved action
- Share of actions that reach full crew size
