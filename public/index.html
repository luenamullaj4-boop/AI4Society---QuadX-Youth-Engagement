Si funksionon · How it works

From scrolling to showing up in three steps

1

Find a spot

Browse the map by neighbourhood or cause. Each hotspot is checked by the municipal youth office before it goes live, so you know the job is real and safe.

2

Claim your place

Join with one tap. You get the meeting point, what to bring and a crew leader's contact. Under 18? We ask for a parent's OK for field work.

3

Show up, log hours

The crew leader confirms attendance on the day. Your hours build into a certificate you can attach to university applications and CVs.
